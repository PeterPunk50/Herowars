### Defect found

**Tail sweep trigger does not match where the tail goes.** `Boss:step` starts the sweep for any target within 14 studs and at least 70 degrees off the nose, but the tail only reaches the area behind the hips (z 2.5 to 14 back, x -6 to +10). A player beside the shoulders or on the far flank sets it off and is never hit, so the boss wastes the move and its 6 s cooldown. Fix options: trigger only for targets inside the measured box, or add a mirrored sweep for the other side. Not changed in this run.
